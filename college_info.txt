College Information Document

About Our College:
Our college was established in 1995 and is located in the heart of the city. We offer undergraduate and graduate programs in Computer Science, Engineering, Business Administration, and Liberal Arts.

Campus Facilities:
- Modern library with over 100,000 books
- State-of-the-art computer labs
- Sports complex with swimming pool and gymnasium
- Student cafeteria serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner
- On-campus hostel accommodation for 500 students

Admission Process:
Applications open in January every year. The admission process includes:
1. Online application submission
2. Entrance examination in March
3. Personal interview in April
4. Results announced in May
